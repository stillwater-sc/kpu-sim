* **[ ] Timing Closure:** 0ps slack at "Worst-Case" corners (SS/Low Voltage/High Temp).

* **[ ] DRC/LVS:** 100% Design Rule Check (DRC) and Layout vs. Schematic (LVS) clean using foundry-certified deck.
* **[ ] MPW/Test Chip Correlation:** Silicon data from M3 and M4 confirms that the simulation models for power and speed are accurate within ±3%.
