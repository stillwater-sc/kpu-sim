# Session: a standalone tile's record (#473)

**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Version:** v0.10 (in progress)
**Issue:** #473, needed to unblock systars#18 and the esg exporter

## 1. Summary

systars' standalone `SystolicTop` has no DRAM and no DMA. The host places its operands in L3. Its
record could not say so: a `LOAD` with no transaction failed E5, and with no `LOAD` the first `MOVE`
named a residency nothing had opened. The format already says it: an `INHERIT` step opens an L3
residency at the program's start, with no transaction, and a `RETAIN` keeps a result in L3. This
adds the documentation, a fixture program, tests, and a hint in E5's message.

## 2. Technical Decisions

- **No format change.** The issue offered three options: a `LOAD` with no transaction when DRAM is
  not modelled, a new `PLACE` kind, or a residency opened by no step. `INHERIT` is option (b) in
  all but name, already in the language (`inherit X`, "resident from the operator before"), in
  the record's kinds, and exempt from E5's one-transaction rule, since it is not a data step.
  Using it changes nothing for any other producer.
- **The hint, not an exemption.** A `LOAD` with no transaction stays refused. Exempting it would let
  a record lose a real DMA silently. With DRAM not modelled, the message says what to write
  instead.
- **Tested end to end, with kpu-sim's own records.** A program of `inherit`, `acc` and `retain` is
  recorded at L-B and L-T1, then DRAM is declared not modelled. In the timed record, the events a
  level with a DMA sends to it (release credits, writeback tokens) are removed, as a standalone tile
  has none. Both records pass.

## 3. Issues Encountered

- The first program kept A and B resident at its end. A program must release, retain or store
  every residency.

## 4. Wrong Decisions

- None.

## 5. Verification

- `test_dflow_standalone.py`, 5 cases:
  - the fixture places with `INHERIT` and keeps with `RETAIN`;
  - its L-B record passes with DRAM not modelled;
  - its timed record passes with DRAM not modelled and no DRAM event;
  - a `LOAD` with no transaction fails E5 and names `INHERIT`;
  - with DRAM modelled the message is unchanged.

  The hint test fails without the hint.
- In CTest as `kpu_run_csp_dflow_standalone` and `dflow_standalone_selftest`.
