**Date:** 2026-10-11

**Status:** Complete (PR 4; the retire-l0-derivations plan is complete with it)
**Tests:** 271/271

## 1. Summary

#360 (PR 3) was merged. The user approved Q4's recommendation: every check of a level's values
against L-B moves to the tile layer (§3.8), L-T1 included; the trace's replay stays bit-exact.

- `tile_check.hpp` gains `check_operands`: every operand of the oracle, per tile, within bound.
  An operand the result lacks is thrown.
- kpu-run reports `within bound (N operands, max abs X)` per level, or the tiles out of bound,
  and exits 1. The bit-for-bit `compare_bitwise` is gone.
- ADR 0001 D5 is amended, §7.5's L-T1 bullet is marked superseded, and `CLAUDE.md`'s fidelity
  table and Authorities paragraph say "within the tile bound of L-B".

## 2. The rule

A comparison is **moved to the tile check** when a different level, or a different schedule,
computes the values. It **stays bit-exact** when it repeats the same computation:
- the trace's replay by `TileProgramReference` (it validates that the trace records its run);
- a serialization round trip, or a corpus file against today's trace at L-B;
- stepping against running at one level;
- one level under a timing-only change (NoC on and off, the DMA window, a vector unit's speed);
- one run through another route (orchestrated vs direct, MMIO vs in-process, cold vs warm).

## 3. Moved, with their bound

Default float32 bound (atol 1e-6, rtol 1e-4) unless stated.
- `tools/kpu-run`: every level against L-B (the 5 MUST_MATCH regexes that named a level now read `within.bound`).
- `test_csp_run`: `check_values` (every level, every operand), the trace-limit test, and L-T1's
  credit test against L-B.
- `test_csp_inherit_retain`: both operators at every level, and the handed-on C.
- `test_csp_corpus`: against the recorded answer (the cross-machine bound, atol 1e-5) and against
  L-B on this host.
- `test_l0_corpus`: against the recorded answer (atol 1e-5); its own `close_enough` is gone.
- `test_csp_language`, `test_csp_structured`: a written program against kpu-csp-gen's program of
  the same operator, a different schedule.
- `test_virtual_platform`: L-T1 against L-B through the platform.
- `test_orchestrator`: the orchestrated run against the composed L-B oracle (L-B and L-T1).
- `test_csp_spec_oracle`: L-CA's C against L-B on S1, T4, T16, T64, with the NoC, in a window,
  and per compute tile. The four `bit_identical` assertions are gone; `check_c` wraps the flat C.
- `test_csp_linear_lca`: every placement against L-B; placements of one activation against each
  other (a different data path: fabric, streamer or BlockMover vector unit); fused against
  unfused at L-B (two schedules); the windowed stream against L-B.

## 4. Kept bit-exact, with why

- `csp_run.hpp`'s replay check, `test_csp_run`'s replay and input-carry checks, `test_csp_gen`'s
  replay, `test_csp_linear`'s replay: the trace must record its run.
- `test_csp_gen` 60, `test_csp_language` 114, `test_csp_structured` 147: L-B on the compiled
  program against L-B on its stream, one computation in two representations.
- `test_csp_step`: stepping against running.
- `test_l0_serialization`, `test_l0_corpus`'s same-host test, `test_driver_run_at`: round trips
  and determinism.
- `test_orchestrator` (orchestrated vs direct, cold vs warm), `test_orchestration_mmio`,
  `test_reservation`: the same run through another route.
- `test_csp_spec_oracle`'s NoC-on against NoC-off and window against tile-level;
  `test_csp_linear_lca`'s slow vector unit against fast: timing-only changes.
- `test_tile_program`: kernels against arithmetic (naive matmul, P·A = L·U), not against L-B.
