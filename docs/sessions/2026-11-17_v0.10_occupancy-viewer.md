# Session: the resource-occupancy viewer, MVP (#390)

**Date:** 2026-11-17
**Version:** v0.10 (in progress)
**Issue:** #390 (first PR: the viewer on #402's timed events)

## 1. Summary

`tools/visualization/occupancy/` puts a timed `.dflow` record on one clock axis:

- process lanes with their transactions;
- L3 and L2 slot lanes with their holdings;
- tile selection;
- an overlay of a second record, joined on the identity key, with a jump to the first divergence.

## 2. Technical Decisions

- **The user's choice:** an MVP on the timed events #402 added, usable now on L-T1 and L-CA
  records, rather than a schema-first or after-#401 start.
- **A separate page**, not a mode of the tile-flow viewer (#390's open question): the axes and
  scales differ. It follows the domain-flow viewer's bundle loading and `pack.py`.
- **Slot holdings from the events alone:** a landing transaction's destination address opens a
  holding, and a credit's source address closes it. No new record table is needed, which is why the
  MVP can read any timed record, the RTL's included.
- **The join is `dflow_diff.py`'s, in the page:** the same key, spelled the same, with a layer
  whose address forms differ joined without its addresses. The test holds the two to the same
  result.

## 3. Issues Encountered

- `dflow_diff.py` is in #470, not yet on `main`, so the join-agreement test skips without it. It
  was checked with it on two pairs: L-CA against L-T1, and L-CA against the RTL sample.

## 4. Wrong Decisions

- None.
