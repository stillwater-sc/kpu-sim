# v0.9 L3 Placement, Step 4: the Trace Carries Placement (L0 Opset 1.2.0)

**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Version:** v0.9
**Status:** Complete (step 4 of 7, `docs/plans/l3-placement-at-lb.md`)
**Tests:** 273/273

## 1. Summary

The L0 trace now records every L3 transaction with its address, so the reference's replay checks
placement as well as values.

- `tile_program.hpp`:
  - `L3Address` moved here from `csp_program.hpp`, which keeps the name with a `using`;
  - seven op kinds: `L3_LOAD`, `L3_STORE`, `L3_MOVE`, `L3_WRITEBACK`, `L3_RELEASE`,
    `L3_INHERIT` and `L3_RETAIN`; `is_l3(kind)`;
  - `TileOp::l3`, with the op's tile in `outputs[0]`.
- `csp/lang/walk.hpp`:
  - `emit` records one L3 op for every action on a residency, and the action's `l0_op` points
    at it. Before, Writeback, Store and Release on the accumulator path borrowed the Drain's
    index.
  - An accumulating call's Feed op is now recorded after its tile's Move, so the trace moves the
    tile out of its slot before it feeds.
- `serialize/l0_format.hpp`:
  - L3 ops are written `out=<tile> l3= slot= bank=`; the reader requires all three and exactly
    one tile, given as `out=`;
  - `opset_version()` and `opset_for` are 1.2.0;
  - `MIN_CONSUMER` is unchanged, and why is written beside `opset_for`.
- `tile_program_reference.hpp`:
  - the replay runs the L3 ops on an addressed L3 of tags, throwing `PlacementError`;
  - `RunSummary::l3_ops` and `peak_l3`, and `l3_contents()`.
- `driver/csp_run.hpp`: `csp_reference` requires the replay to agree with L-B on placement: no
  refusal, the same peak, the same slots held at the end (`BehavioralInterpreter::l3_contents()`).
- **The compute-schedule analyses skip the L3 ops:**
  - `apply` (no-op);
  - `tile_work_of` and `l1_duration` (no work);
  - `build_tile_dependencies` (no hazards);
  - `peak_live_tiles`, `Metrics::ops` and the chrome trace;
  - `CspDriver`'s check of the trace's ops.

## 2. Decisions

- **One L3 op per action on a residency (the user, on review).** The plan's draft had four ops,
  plus `from=l3:slot` on Feed. That leaves reads unchecked: LU's in-place calls record no Feed,
  and Store and Inherit had no op. A new field on an existing op would also be dropped silently
  by a 1.1 reader. New ops are refused by name, so `MIN_CONSUMER` stays as it is.
- **The analyses skip the L3 ops (the user, on review).** They model the compute schedule. Slot
  hazards are timed at L-T1 in step 5, so the dependency DAG gives L3 ops no edges (tested), and
  they cost nothing.
- **The tile is in `outputs`, for every L3 op.** `program_inputs` counts an operand in any op's
  `inputs` as read and fills it. A Store's tile in `inputs` would have filled C. With `outputs`,
  `program_inputs` is unchanged, and so are the kernel files' answers.
- **The replay's placement check is a comparison with L-B, not only self-consistency:** the same
  peak and the same slots held at the end. Its refusals are the ones L-B makes.

## 3. Wrong decisions

- **A test assumed LU's trace has Feeds.** My new test looked for the first Feed in LU's trace,
  and LU's in-place calls record none. I limited the Feed-order check to matmul, and commented
  why.
- **My Catch2 filter ran nothing.** To dump the loadable's bytes, I passed a test name containing
  a comma; Catch2 reads a comma as a separator, so the case never ran and no file was written. I
  reran the whole binary. The temporary dump was reverted (`git checkout`) before the fixture was
  copied and the test rerun.
- **An edit script's anchor was not unique** (`### Added` occurs ten times in the CHANGELOG). The
  script asserts, so nothing was written to that file; the rerun anchored on `## [Unreleased]`.

  above:
  - `test_l0_corpus` (corpus not yet regenerated);

  `OPSET` removed, every `.l0` equals its previous version. No `.result.l0` changed.
- **No `-Wswitch` warning** in the full build (CI builds with `-Werror`).
- New tests (`test_csp_lb_place.cpp`, 3 cases):
  - every placed action has its own L3 op with its address and tile, and L-B's trace holds the
    same count;
  - a Feed follows its tile's `L3_MOVE`;
  - L3 ops round-trip through the text byte for byte, and the reader refuses one without
    `slot=` or with `in=`;
  - the replay refuses #279's wrong-slot release, a load into a live slot, a double release, and
    a slot never freed.
