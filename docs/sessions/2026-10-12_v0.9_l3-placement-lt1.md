# v0.9 L3 Placement, Step 5a: L-T1 Executes Placement (a Credit Is a Slot)

**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Version:** v0.9
**Status:** Complete (step 5a of 7, `docs/plans/l3-placement-at-lb.md`; 5b is `.tflow`'s slot binding)
**Tests:** see §4 (new: `test_csp_lt1_place`, 3 cases)

## 1. Summary

L-T1 used anonymous L3 credits: a priority queue of the times each free slot frees, so a Load took
whichever slot freed first. Now every action that opens a residency (Load, Inherit, opening
Writeback) waits for its own slot's Release, the slot the walker placed it at. A program or a
policy that reuses a slot early serializes on it, as a register's write-after-read does.

- `csp/transactional.hpp`:
  - `slot_free_[(module, slot)]` replaces `credits_`; `take_slot(a)` returns when the action's
    slot is free;
  - `Residency::place`; `Slot::place`; `Record::place` (every leg names its slot);
  - the constructor takes the program's banks, and passes them to the value interpreter, so
    L-T1 refuses a wrong bank as L-B does;
  - `Stats::slot_bound` and `slot_wait_cycles`: the Loads whose start their slot decided (it freed
    after the data was ready, a DMA lane was free and in-order issue allowed), and what those
    waits cost.
- `driver/csp_run.hpp`: passes `ast.banks`; `CspLevelOutcome::slot_bound` and `slot_wait_cycles`.
- `driver/csp_step.hpp`: the L-T1 replay stepper sets `CspStep::place`, so `kpu-run --step` names
  the slot at L-T1 too.
- `kpu-run`: the L-T1 L3 line adds `slot-bound N (W cycles)`.

## 2. Decisions

- **Step 5 is split.** 5a is the executor (this PR). 5b is `.tflow` binding slots: a residency
  column in the record (format v5), the viewer and `tflow_check.py`. 5b changes a file format and
  a JS viewer, so it gets its own review.
- **A new metric, rather than a reinterpretation of `credit_stalls`.** `credit_stalls` counts a
  Load whose slot freed after its data was ready, even when the DMA lane was not free either. My
  first tests used it and found nothing (§3). `slot_bound` counts only the Loads the slot
  actually delayed. `credit_stalls` keeps its meaning, so existing outputs do not shift.
- **The tests time on kpu_s1's lanes.** On `DeviceDescriptor::single()`, the one DMA lane is
  always the bound, so a slot never decides a start, and placement cannot show.

## 3. Wrong decisions

- **My first measurement used the wrong metric and the wrong device.** I asserted that pinning a
  stream to two slots raises `credit_stalls` and the makespan, on the single-site device. The
  makespans were equal (1344 both), because the single DMA lane was the binding constraint
  throughout. That led to the `slot_bound` metric and to kpu_s1's lanes, where the effect is
  real: 704 wait cycles and a makespan of 964 pinned, against 416 and 740 free.
- **The plan's expectation was wrong for kpu-csp-gen's matmul.** It expected `lowest` to show
  more slot waits than `fifo`. On matmul 128/32 neither policy has a slot-bound Load, at its own
  L3 or at twice it, and the makespans are equal: its Releases come early enough that a reused
  slot is always free by the time the DMA reaches the Load. The streamed program shows the
  effect, with `lowest` equal to pinning. Recorded in the plan's step 5 and in the test.

  - the matmul measurement is printed.
