# v0.10 Synthesized Inputs Keep an Operand's Domain (#457)

**Date:** 2026-11-17
**Version:** v0.10
**Status:** in review.

## 1. Summary

`kpu-run` synthesized every operand across its type's whole range, so a per-channel requantized
program's shift vector held values like -1811849867, and L-B refused the first Drain. Such a
program could not be run, viewed or checked without hand-written `--inputs`. Synthesis now keeps
the domain each operand's role has, read from the program.

## 2. Technical Decisions

- **Decision 1: roles from the program, never from names.** `operand_roles` walks the parsed
  program for a per-channel `scale(qm[j], qs[j])` (two vector arguments) and gives its first
  vector the Multiplier role and its second the Shift role, whatever they are called. The roles
  and their domains are listed in one place, `driver::OperandRole`, as the issue asked.
- **Decision 2: domains that exercise the arithmetic**, not just ones that pass:
  - a multiplier in [2^14, 2^15) and a shift in [22, 28];
  - an int8 matmul's accumulator (|x| up to about 2^20) lands across the int8 result: on the
    64x64 run, 248 of 256 values occur and about 15% saturate, so rounding and clamping are both
    exercised;
  - per element and deterministic, as fill_operand is, so a run is the same on every host.
- **Decision 3: the tests that pinned the refusal now expect the run.** The CTest
  `kpu_run_csp_requant_channels_needs_inputs` (renamed `..._synthesized`) and a check in
  `test_csp_requant` asserted the old refusal. They now expect the run, and the refusal of an
  out-of-range shift is still tested, with an explicit shift of 70.

## 3. Issues Encountered

- None beyond the two pinned tests.

## 4. Wrong Decisions

- None.

## 5. Verification

- `test_csp_requant` "synthesized inputs keep a per-channel scale's domains, and every level runs
  on them":
  - the roles are qm and qs only, and the values lie in their domains with the channels differing;
  - on os, ws and is, L-B, L-T1 and L-CA all run on them and agree with L-B.
- CTests `kpu_run_requant_channels_synthesized` (a generated int8 matmul) and
  `kpu_run_csp_requant_channels_synthesized` (the linear) run at every level with no `--inputs`.
- A sweep of `kpu-csp-gen`'s options (algo, dataflow, dtype, init, requant, per channel, place):
  every one of the 34 programs it writes runs clean under `kpu-run --level all`. The other 182
  combinations the generator itself refuses.
- Full suites, `release-werror`, gcc and clang.
