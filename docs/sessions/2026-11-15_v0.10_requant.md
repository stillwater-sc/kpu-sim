# Session 2026-11-15: the Combiner's requantization, int32 -> int8 (#427 part 2b)

## Context

#427 part 2a (#436, the MX block formats) is in review. Part 2b is esg#68's scaling: "a
per-tile or per-channel scale and shift (requantization, int32 -> int8), with rounding and
saturation". This builds the per-tile form.

## Decisions

1. **A result declares its accumulator:** `tensor C[..] int8 tile .. out acc int32`
   (`TensorOperand::acc`, `acc_type()`).
   - The tile's words hold accumulator values until the scale, and storage values after it.
     This is the hardware's view: the result in L2 and DRAM is int8, the accumulation int32.
   - Alternatives considered:
     - a second tensor that the store renames into (`store C via scale into Q`). It needs tile
       renaming at every level.
     - an implicit accumulator for any int8 result. That hides the number system, which the
       language states everywhere else.
2. **The arithmetic: `round(x * m / 2^shift)`, ties to even, saturated to the target type**,
   computed exactly in int64 (|m| < 2^31, shift 0..62).
   - esg#68 does not fix the rounding mode. Ties to even matches the block formats' rounding,
     and is unbiased.
   - **This is the architecture's choice, and the RTL implements it** (corrected the same day:
     kpu-sim leads, the RTL follows). Common alternatives are round half up (add then shift: the
     cheapest hardware) and gemmlowp's half away from zero.
3. **Strict typing, for now.**
   - A requantized result's context is exactly `scale(m, shift) @ combiner`, on every path
     into storage: a fabric accumulator's store, an L2 accumulator's final merge.
   - The tensor is `out`, never resident, retained or stored late.
   - No int8 bias or activation (the activation kernels are fp32), and no per-channel scale.
     Each is a later step.
4. **The trace:** `REQUANT m= shift=` (opset 1.7), and `OPERAND acc=`. By the format's own rule,
   `acc=` carries semantics, so a file with it demands container 1.4.0 (MIN_CONSUMER 1.4.0). An
   older reader would read int32 words as int8. Files without it are unchanged.
5. **A target unit lists `scale` among its ops,** like any stage. The test machine's Combiner
   declares it.

## What changed

- **Operand, registry and kernels:** `TensorOperand::acc`; `numeric::requantize`.
  - The gemm, merge and init kernels compute in `acc_type()`.
  - New: `requant_tile` and `kernel_requant`.
- **L0 format:** REQUANT, `OPERAND acc=`, and the version rules.
- **Language:**
  - `acc` in declarations;
  - `scale(m, shift)`, with integer arguments, in the parser and formatter;
  - `VeOp::Scale`, and its order and place rules in the resolver;
  - the typing rules in the walker and the validator.
- **Levels:**
  - L-B: `stages()`, and merges in `acc_type()`;
  - L-CA: `VectorStage::Op::Scale` with its types; merges and the MAC in `acc_type()`;
  - `csp_inputs` and `compile` carry `acc`.
- **Generator and tool:** `Options::requant`, `--requant m,shift`.
- **Tests:** `tests/program/test_csp_requant.cpp`, 5 cases:
  - the registry's rounding and saturation;
  - the programs and their round trip;
  - the refusals, in the walker and the validator;
  - OS, WS and IS at every level against an independent reference;
  - the L0 round trip.

## Wrong decisions

  was right to refuse it, and the fix was to the test's machine.
