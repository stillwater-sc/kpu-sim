# Session 2026-11-15: the reuse and bubble report (#425, first item)

## Context

#427's parts 1, 2a and 2b merged (#435, #436, #437). Part 3, the RTL contracts, is blocked:
#427 asks for it to move in step with esg#67 and esg#68, both still open, and kpu-sim's tile
command lowering (#397) is open too.

#425 tracks the CSP data-movement view. Most of its items are other issues on later milestones
(#402, #390, #286, #391, #392, #405). Its own first item depends on none of them: for every
call, when each operand arrives against when the array needs it; for every operand, the reuse
the program states; and the bandwidth needed against what is supplied. It starts at L-T1. This
session builds that report.

## Decisions

1. **Built from L-T1's records, with no causal links in the interpreter.**
   - Each record gains `ready`: the cycle its inputs and its credit were there.
   - The language's lowering puts a call's Moves and Feeds right before it, so the walk from a
     call to its operand's Feed, Move and Load is by tile and program order.
2. **A wait is a lane's only when its process was saturated** (every lane busy over 90% of the
   wait). Otherwise the action was held behind the one before it in its process's in-order issue,
   a head-of-line block, and the walk follows that action.
   - **Wrong decision:** my first version blamed any late start on a busy lane. With one slow DMA
     lane, 99% busy, it blamed the BlockMover, which was 1% busy. Its Moves were queued behind a
     Move whose Load was late. A record dump showed it. The saturation test and the head-of-line
     walk fixed it, and the test now checks both the DMA-bound and the DRAM-bound cases.
3. **Reuse as two ratios per operand:** feeds per load (across DRAM) and feeds per move (across
   L3: an L2 staging or a stationary operand).
4. **Text first.** `kpu-run --bubbles` prints the report. A viewer pane (#390, #391) and the
   L-CA version are later items of #425.
