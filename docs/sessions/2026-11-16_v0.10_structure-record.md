**Date:** 2026-11-16
**Version:** v0.10

## 1. Summary

ADR 0005 puts every view of the machine in kpu-sim, and the formats first. Two viewers already
needed one, the floorplan views moved here from esg (#447, #448), so this is the first:
- one record of a machine's structure and floorplan, written by the model or by an RTL
  generator;
- a spec, a schema and a checker;
- a converter from both existing floorplan formats, and samples from both producers.

## 3. Technical Decisions

- **Decision 1: one record for the structure and the floorplan.** #392 had specified the
  structure with no coordinates, and each RTL repository drawing its own SVG. ADR 0005 puts the
  floorplan views here, so the floorplan is an optional layer of the same record, rather than a
  second format beside it.
- **Decision 2: mm, origin at the die's lower-left.** These are the RTL's units and its physical
  frame. The model converts from its illustrative µm pitch and flags the result
  `floorplan.estimated`.
- **Decision 3: a flat block list linked by `parent`, with stable ids.** It is esg's form, and it
  is what `.dflow` events will reference. Ids are each producer's own names: RTL instance paths
  (kept in `rtl_path` too), or naming-map resources (kept in `resource`).
- **Decision 4: the KPU model's block vocabulary.** esg's kinds map onto it: `checkerboard` to
  `array`, `memory_cell` to `l3_tile`, `systolic`, `compute` and `fixed_function` to
  `compute_tile` with their class. The original kind is kept in the block's notes.
- **Decision 5: networks carry their routing rule** (`xy`, `ring`, `tree`), so a view derives a
  route between two nodes and never hard-codes one (esg#61's lenses).
- **Decision 6: a checker without dependencies, and a schema beside it.** The RTL repositories
  pin both. The checker checks what a schema cannot: references, the tree, a block inside its
  parent, the area breakdown, a link's ends at its nodes.

## 4. Issues Encountered

- The esg clone's `kpu_edge_hetero` floorplan was a build from before esg's rename
  (`kpugen-floorplan`). Both samples were regenerated with the current kpu-gen (756039f) into the
  scratchpad, then converted.
- The first CTest chain used `DEPENDS` for the floorplan test. That is #442's review finding
  again, caught before the push: it is a fixture chain now, and `ctest -R structure_check_t4`
  alone runs `kpu_floorplan_t4` first.
