# Session: two L-CA characterizations at the deployment's rates and banks (#462)

**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Version:** v0.10 (in progress)
**Issue:** #462

## 1. Summary

#458 mapped the deployment's mover and streamer rates into L-CA, and pinned two characterizations
to the old rates, for this issue to explain. #401 then banked L2. Measured at each deployment's own
rates, with banks, both claims are restated and unpinned.

## 2. Findings

**(1) The NoC, 128³ in 32³ tiles, wired against not wired:**

| deployment | legacy rates, pooled | deployment rates, pooled | deployment rates, banked |
|---|---|---|---|
| T4 | +1.10% | −1.95% | +1.87% |
| T16 | +3.50% | +4.82% | +5.19% |
| T64 | +6.43% | +5.95% | +6.90% |

T4's "faster with the NoC" appears only on the pooled L2 at the deployment's rates. With banks,
the fabric costs time on all three, so "the fabric costs time; it does not save any" holds
unpinned. I read the pooled reversal as an issue-order effect: the NoC delays loads, and on a
pooled L2 that changes the order the movers take credits in, as the window effect in #424 does.
I haven't traced that order directly; the banked results are the evidence.

**(2) The driver window, S1, 128³ in 32³ tiles,** each window against the whole program:

| window | legacy, pooled | deployment, pooled | deployment, banked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | −0.7% | +11.2% | +9.6% |
| 64 | +1.4% | −2.2% | −2.3% |
| 256 | +0.6% | +0.1% | +0.03% |

At S1's movers lookahead matters more, so the threshold moves from 16 to 64. The tolerance is 3%:
the worst from 64 up is 2.3%, and MSVC's DMA timing differs by a cycle or two (seen in #471).

## 3. Decisions

- Unpin rather than loosen: the claims are measured and restated at the deployment's rates and
  banks, with their numbers in the test comments.
- The legacy-rate and pooled-L2 pins are removed with their constants.

- None.
