KPU Tile-Flow Viewer

Where every tile was, and when: the system-level schedule of one run, on the physical floorplan and as station swimlanes, with one shared cursor.

Load a run

Write a bundle with kpu-run --tflow run.tflow, then choose the folder below (or drop its files here). For the physical view, also choose the deployment's floorplan from kpu-floorplan --deploy spec.json --json fp.json; for per-resource rows, run bind.py run.tflow --floorplan fp.json first.
