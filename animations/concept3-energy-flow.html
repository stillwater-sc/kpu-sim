The GPU Tax: Every single FMA operation costs ~100 pJ, but only 12 pJ does useful work. 88% of energy is wasted on instruction infrastructure - fetching, decoding, scheduling, and shuffling data through the register file.

The Domain Flow Advantage: No instructions to fetch or decode. No scheduler. No banked register file. Data flows directly through compute. 83% of energy does useful work. Same compute for 6.7× less total energy.
